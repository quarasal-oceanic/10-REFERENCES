## Overview

This section documents marine ecosystems, biodiversity, ecological interactions, and environmental impacts associated with underwater infrastructure.

The objective is to understand how underwater construction and human activities can coexist with marine ecosystems while minimizing environmental disturbance.

## 1. Core Research Areas

### 1.1 Marine Biodiversity

- Marine species distribution.
- Coral reef ecosystems.
- Deep-sea biodiversity.
- Habitat relationships.
- Ecological monitoring.

### 1.2 Marine Environmental Impact

- Construction-related disturbances.
- Seabed habitat alteration.
- Noise pollution.
- Artificial lighting.
- Water quality changes.

### 1.3 Artificial Marine Habitats

- Artificial reefs.
- Marine habitat restoration.
- Ecological colonization.
- Subsea structures and biodiversity.
- Marine ecosystem interactions.

### 1.4 Sustainable Underwater Infrastructure

- Environmentally responsible construction.
- Marine ecosystem protection.
- Wastewater management.
- Pollution prevention.
- Long-term ecological monitoring.

## 3. Research Questions

1. How can underwater infrastructure minimize disturbance to existing marine ecosystems?
2. Can carefully designed subsea structures support artificial marine habitats?
3. How should marine biodiversity be monitored around underwater settlements?
4. What wastewater and waste-treatment systems are necessary to prevent ecological damage?
5. How can long-term environmental impacts be measured?

## Central Research Question

**How can underwater infrastructure coexist with marine ecosystems while maintaining long-term environmental sustainability?**
