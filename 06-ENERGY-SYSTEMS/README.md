## Overview

This section documents renewable energy technologies, power generation, storage, and distribution systems relevant to underwater infrastructure.

The objective is to investigate reliable energy sources capable of supporting continuous underwater operations and habitation.

## 1. Core Research Areas

### 1.1 Offshore Wind Energy

- Offshore wind turbines.
- Floating wind platforms.
- Subsea power transmission.
- Electrical grid integration.

### 1.2 Tidal Energy

- Tidal stream turbines.
- Tidal range systems.
- Current-based energy generation.
- Underwater turbine technologies.

### 1.3 Wave Energy

- Wave energy converters.
- Oscillating water columns.
- Point absorbers.
- Floating wave energy systems.

### 1.4 Ocean Thermal Energy Conversion (OTEC)

- Temperature differences between surface and deep water.
- Closed-cycle OTEC.
- Open-cycle OTEC.
- Hybrid thermal energy systems.

### 1.5 Energy Storage and Distribution

- Battery storage.
- Subsea electrical infrastructure.
- Power management.
- Redundant electrical systems.
- Emergency power supplies.

## 3. Research Questions

1. Which renewable energy technologies are suitable for underwater infrastructure?
2. How can power be transmitted safely to underwater habitats?
3. What energy storage capacity is required for continuous operation?
4. How should power systems respond to prolonged external supply failures?
5. Can multiple marine energy sources be integrated into a reliable power network?

## Central Research Question

**How can a reliable, resilient, and sustainable energy infrastructure support long-term underwater habitation?**
