## Overview

This section documents underwater robotics, autonomous systems, remote inspection technologies, and automated maintenance solutions for subsea infrastructure.

Robotics can reduce the need for human intervention in dangerous underwater environments and improve the reliability of long-term operations.

## 1. Core Research Areas

### 1.1 Remotely Operated Vehicles (ROVs)

- Remote subsea inspection.
- Manipulator systems.
- Underwater maintenance.
- Structural monitoring.

### 1.2 Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs)

- Autonomous navigation.
- Seabed mapping.
- Environmental monitoring.
- Infrastructure inspection.

### 1.3 Underwater Manipulation

- Robotic arms.
- Subsea assembly.
- Component replacement.
- Repair operations.

### 1.4 Artificial Intelligence

- Autonomous decision-making.
- Defect detection.
- Predictive maintenance.
- Sensor data analysis.
- Fault diagnosis.

### 1.5 Underwater Communication

- Acoustic communication.
- Optical communication.
- Underwater wireless networks.
- Navigation and localization.

## 3. Research Questions

1. Which underwater maintenance tasks can be automated?
2. How can autonomous robots inspect pressure-resistant structures?
3. How can underwater robots identify and respond to structural defects?
4. What communication infrastructure is required for autonomous operations?
5. How can human operators supervise multiple autonomous underwater systems?

## Central Research Question

**How can autonomous underwater robotics improve the safety, efficiency, and reliability of subsea infrastructure?**
