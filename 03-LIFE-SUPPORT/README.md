## Overview

This section documents technologies and scientific research related to environmental control and life-support systems for underwater habitats.

The primary objective is to understand how a controlled living environment can maintain breathable air, water availability, temperature, humidity, and waste management under isolated underwater conditions.

## 1. Environmental Control and Life Support System (ECLSS)

### 1.1 NASA ECLSS

NASA's Environmental Control and Life Support System provides a technical reference for maintaining human life in isolated and enclosed environments.

**Research Focus:**
- Atmospheric pressure regulation.
- Oxygen generation and management.
- Carbon dioxide removal.
- Water recovery and recycling.
- Waste management.
- Temperature and humidity control.

**References:**
- [NASA — Environmental Control and Life Support Systems](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/environmental-control-and-life-support-systems-eclss/)
- [NASA — ECLSS Technical Brief](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/eclss-technical-brief-ochmo.pdf)

### 1.2 NASA Life Support Subsystems

Technical documentation of individual life-support subsystems used in spacecraft and the International Space Station.

**Research Focus:**
- Air revitalization.
- Water processing.
- Oxygen generation.
- Carbon dioxide removal.
- Waste processing.

**Reference:**
- [NASA — Life Support Subsystems](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/jsc-life-support-subsystems/)

### 1.3 Designing for Human Presence in Space — NASA RP-1324

A technical reference concerning environmental requirements and life-support considerations for human habitation in space.

**Research Focus:**
- Human environmental requirements.

## 2. Core Research Areas

- Oxygen generation and storage.
- Carbon dioxide removal.
- Atmospheric monitoring.
- Humidity and temperature regulation.
- Drinking water production.
- Water recycling and purification.
- Wastewater treatment.
- Solid waste management.
- Food production and storage.
- Emergency life-support reserves.

## 3. Closed-Loop Life Support

Closed-loop life-support systems aim to recover and reuse essential resources, reducing dependence on external supply chains.

Important research topics include:

- Water recovery.
- Nutrient recycling.
- Biological waste processing.
- Resource recovery.
- Air revitalization.
- Integration with controlled agriculture.

## 4. Research Questions

1. How can a reliable breathable atmosphere be maintained in an isolated underwater habitat?
2. What technologies can provide continuous oxygen generation?
3. How can water recovery and recycling efficiency be improved?
4. How should life-support systems respond to equipment failure?
5. What level of resource independence is technically achievable?

## Central Research Question

**How can an integrated and reliable life-support system sustain human habitation in an isolated underwater environment?**
